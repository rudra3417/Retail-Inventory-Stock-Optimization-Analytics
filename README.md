# Retail Inventory & Stock Optimization Analytics

## Project Objective
To analyze retail sales and inventory data, evaluate business performance, identify inventory issues such as overstock and stockouts, and generate insights to support better inventory management decisions.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Formulas and Functions
- XLOOKUP
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel Dashboard and Slicers

## Dataset
Used a synthetic retail dataset containing sales transactions, product details, and inventory records to represent a retail business scenario.

## Project Workflow
1. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent entries, and data type issues.
2. **Data Preparation:** Used Excel formulas and XLOOKUP to retrieve related product information and create calculated fields.
3. **KPI Calculation:** Calculated Total COGS, Average Inventory Value, and Inventory Turnover to evaluate inventory performance.
4. **Data Analysis:** Used PivotTables and PivotCharts to analyze sales trends, category performance, store performance, and inventory status.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Built an interactive Excel dashboard with KPI cards, charts, and Month and Store slicers.

## Key Analysis
- Monthly sales trends and category-wise sales performance
- Sales comparison across stores
- Top 5 products based on sales
- Inventory distribution across Healthy Stock, Overstock, Reorder Required, and Stockout
- Top 5 products based on inventory turnover

## Key Findings
- Electronics was the highest-selling category.
- Four of the top five products by sales were Samsung 55-inch TV variants.
- 42.58% of inventory records were classified as Overstock.
- 15.19% of inventory records were classified as Reorder Required.

## Business Recommendations
- Review overstocked inventory to reduce unnecessary stock holding.
- Monitor products requiring reorder to support timely replenishment.
- Pay close attention to high-selling products to maintain stock availability.
